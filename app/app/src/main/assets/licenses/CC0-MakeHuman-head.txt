Head template (head_template.json)

Derived from the MakeHuman base mesh "hm08" (makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj,
https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman).

The base mesh was explicitly released under CC0 1.0 Universal in September 2020 by its
copyright holders (Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius, Jonas Hauquier). See
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Changes: head and neck cut from the body, fitted to MediaPipe's canonical face model,
UVs, regions and landmark correspondence added (tools/head_template.py).
